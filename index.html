A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you start.

What is free?

Free includes 20 voice or typed captures, 3 research runs, and 5 generated day plans each month. Manually adding and editing tasks, managing habits, and editing your schedule are included. One capture can create several tasks.

What does Unpact Pro add?

Unpact Pro removes those monthly capture, research, and generated-plan limits. See the available subscriptions, local prices, and billing periods in the mobile app. Manage or cancel through the store where you subscribe.

Can I type instead of speaking?

Yes. You can type on mobile and Windows. In-app voice recording is available on mobile; Windows uses typed capture.

Can I change the tasks Unpact creates?

Yes. Once your capture is processed, open a task to change its title, date, or details. Capture creates tasks automatically; a generated day plan is a separate draft you choose to accept.

Which devices can I use?

Download the Android beta or Windows app below. For current iPhone access, get in touch. Voice recording, reminders, and alarms are mobile features; Windows supports typed capture.

What happens to recordings and transcripts?

Opening capture history removes finished memos older than 14 days, including their recordings and memo transcripts. Tasks and task-processing records, including a copy of the input text, remain with your account. This window does not describe provider retention. See our privacy policy and account deletion instructions for details.
