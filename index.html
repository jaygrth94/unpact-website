QUESTIONS

A few useful details.

Can I change what the AI creates?

Yes. Tasks, titles, dates, durations, subtasks, priorities, reminders, and plans remain editable.

Does every task get subtasks?

No. Unpact can suggest subtasks when they make the work clearer, but it keeps genuinely simple tasks simple.

What is the difference between a notification and an alarm?

Notifications are ordinary device reminders. Alarms are stronger native alerts available only on supported mobile builds and require the relevant device permissions.

Can I use Unpact in a browser?

The browser experience is being prepared as a login-based portal. It does not need to be installed; native phone integrations will continue to live in the mobile apps.

Is my data private?

Your tasks and memos belong to you. You can export your data or delete your account. Read the privacy policy for details.

Does it work offline?

AI capture and current account synchronization require a connection. Unpact does not currently promise full offline operation.
